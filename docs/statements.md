# Statements for preprint submission

## Ethics statement

This study used only the Canadian Community Health Survey (CCHS) 2017-2018 Public Use Microdata File, which Statistics Canada releases publicly under the Statistics Act and the Statistics Canada Open Licence after removing direct identifiers and applying disclosure control. No individual could be identified and no data linkage was performed. Under the Tri-Council Policy Statement: Ethical Conduct for Research Involving Humans (TCPS 2, 2022), research that relies exclusively on information that is publicly available through a mechanism set out by legislation or regulation and protected by law (Article 2.2), or on secondary use of anonymous information where data linkage, recording or dissemination of results does not generate identifiable information (Article 2.4), does not require research ethics board review. No ethics review was sought.

## Data availability statement

The CCHS 2017-2018 Public Use Microdata File, its bootstrap weights and documentation are freely available from Statistics Canada (Canadian Community Health Survey: Public Use Microdata File, 2000 to 2022, catalogue 82M0013X2024001, https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/82m0013x/82m0013x2024001-eng.htm) under the Statistics Canada Open Licence.
